OPERATIONAL BRIEFING — READ BEFORE REPORTING FOR DUTY

THE DATE IS JANUARY 1942.

The United States has entered the Second World War. The Pacific is in crisis. Japan possesses one of the world's most powerful naval forces and has seized the initiative across the Pacific.

You are entering advanced training for duty as a carrier task group commander. Your command will depend on aircraft, ships, intelligence, communications, timing, and judgment.

This is not simply a memorization test. You will be given incomplete information and forced to make decisions under pressure.

QUALIFICATION STANDARD: 8 OUT OF 10 ON EVERY TRAINING CHALLENGE.

Fail? Review the briefing and try again. Pass? Your qualification is recorded and the next stage opens.

OFFICER RECORD



Command rule: Protect the carrier. Preserve combat power. Make decisions before the enemy makes them for you.

Historical note: WWII U.S. Navy forces were organized into task forces and task groups. This game uses “carrier task group” rather than the modern term “carrier strike group.”
